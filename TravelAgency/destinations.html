Our Destinations

Explore some of our most exciting travel destinations:

Goa

Experience the vibrant beaches and nightlife of Goa.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Calangute Beach, Dona Paula, Shiwalik Hills

Manali

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Manali's snow-capped mountains and serene valleys.

Best Time to Visit: October to June

Attractions: Hidimba Temple, Vashisht Hot Springs, Manali Mall

Kerala

Immerse yourself in the lush greenery and backwater landscapes of Kerala.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Alleppey Backwaters, Munnar Tea Gardens, Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary

Jaipur

Explore the rich history and vibrant culture of Jaipur, the Pink City.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar

Shimla

Experience the charm of Shimla's colonial architecture and scenic beauty.

Best Time to Visit: October to June

Attractions: Viceregal Lodge, Christ Church, The Mall

Hampi

Discover the ancient ruins and cultural heritage of Hampi.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Virupaksha Temple, Vittala Temple, Stone Chariot

Darjeeling

Experience the beauty of Darjeeling's tea gardens and the majestic Himalayan views.

Best Time to Visit: October to May

Attractions: Tiger Hill, Darjeeling Railway, Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park

Ladakh

Experience the breathtaking landscapes and unique culture of Ladakh.

Best Time to Visit: April to October

Attractions: Pashupatinath Temple, Swayambhunath Stupa, Boudhanath Stupa

Ooty

Experience the charm of Ooty's hill station and colonial architecture.

Best Time to Visit: October to June

Attractions: Hill-station, Botanical Gardens, Tea Estate

Rishikesh

Experience the spiritual ambiance and adventure activities of Rishikesh.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Beatles' House, Ram Jhula, Triveni Ghat

Agara

Experience the historical significance and architectural beauty of Agara.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Agara Fort, Palace of the Nawabs, Local Markets

Bijapur

Experience the historical significance and architectural beauty of Bijapur.

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Attractions: Bijapur Fort, Palace of the Nawabs, Gol Gumbaz
